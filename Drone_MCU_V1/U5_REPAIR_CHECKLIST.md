## U5 Gerber check 2026-11-07

KiCad MCP exported the saved board to the Codex workspace `u5-gerber-check` for inspection; these are review files, not a release package. F.Mask contains 14 U5 flashes, with 0.575 x 0.350 mm rounded-rectangle openings (rotated for the other edge) and 0.150 mm nominal same-row solder-mask webs on the 0.5 mm pitch. F.Paste contains 14 rectangular apertures of 0.455 x 0.230 mm (rotated for the other edge), about 88.1% of the 0.475 x 0.250 mm copper area. The F.Silkscreen Gerber contains the pin-1 circle. No layer geometry was altered during this check.

For a rectangular aperture, area ratio = L*W / (2*(L+W)*stencil thickness): approximately 0.764 at 0.10 mm thickness and 0.637 at 0.12 mm, before fabrication tolerances. Confirm stencil thickness, paste process and aperture acceptance with the assembler. Inspect the mask around the pin-1 mark and adjacent via in a Gerber viewer before release. This does not resolve copper beneath U5, J2 annular width, the other DRC warnings or bring-up review.
